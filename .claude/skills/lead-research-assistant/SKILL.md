# Lead Research Assistant

This skill helps sales and business development professionals identify qualified potential customers by analyzing products/services and finding matching companies.

## Core Functionality

1. **Business Analysis**: Examines your product's value proposition and target market
2. **Company Identification**: Locates firms matching your ideal customer profile based on industry, size, location, and technology use
3. **Lead Prioritization**: Ranks prospects using fit scores and relevance metrics
4. **Outreach Planning**: Delivers customized engagement approaches for each lead
5. **Research Enhancement**: Collects details about key decision-makers and organizational context

## Primary Use Cases

- Prospecting potential customers or partnership candidates
- Researching accounts aligned with your business model
- Compiling contact lists for sales campaigns
- Preparing business development initiatives

## Key Output Features

For each identified lead, the skill provides:
- Company name, website, and industry classification
- Fit assessment scoring (1-10 scale)
- Relevant decision-maker titles
- Tailored value propositions addressing their specific challenges
- Personalized outreach recommendations
- LinkedIn profile links where available

## Optimal Usage Pattern

For better results, provide:
- Specific details about your product
- Clear customer profile definition
- Geographic or industry preferences
- Target audience pain points

Running the tool from your product's source code directory enables automatic context analysis.
