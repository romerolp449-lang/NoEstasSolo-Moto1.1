# Content Research Writer

This skill is a collaborative writing assistant designed to help you research, outline, draft, and refine content while preserving your unique voice.

## Key Features

- **Collaborative outlining** to structure ideas coherently
- **Research assistance** with citations and credible sources
- **Hook improvement** to strengthen openings
- **Section-by-section feedback** during the writing process
- **Voice preservation** maintaining your writing style
- **Citation management** in multiple formats
- **Iterative refinement** through multiple drafts

## How It Works

Start by clarifying your project through questions about:
- Topic and angle
- Target audience
- Desired length
- Goals and objectives
- Sources already available
- Writing style preferences

The assistant then helps you build an outline, identifies research gaps, reviews each section as you write, and provides comprehensive final feedback.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Create a dedicated folder for your article
2. Develop an outline collaboratively
3. Conduct targeted research with citations
4. Strengthen your introduction hook
5. Write and receive feedback section-by-section
6. Polish the complete draft for publication

## Pro Tips

- Work incrementally: write one section, get feedback, then continue
- Keep research in a separate file
- Use version control (draft-v1, draft-v2)
- Read your work aloud to catch awkward phrasing

## Supported Content Types

- Blog posts
- Newsletters
- Technical tutorials
- Thought leadership pieces
- Long-form articles
- Research summaries
