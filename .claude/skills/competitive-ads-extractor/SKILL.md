# Competitive Ads Extractor

This skill gathers and analyzes competitor advertising strategies across platforms to help you understand what positioning and creative strategies competitors are using.

## Core Functions

1. **Extract ads** from ad libraries (Facebook, LinkedIn)
2. **Capture visual copies** of competitor creatives
3. **Analyze messaging patterns** across campaigns
4. **Identify successful creative approaches** and patterns

## Key Use Cases

- Researching competitor ad strategies
- Finding inspiration for your own ads
- Identifying successful ad patterns
- Extracting ads from specific competitors
- Analyzing messaging around particular pain points
- Conducting competitive set analysis across multiple companies

## Analysis Output

The skill generates detailed reports identifying:
- Problems competitors are highlighting
- Use cases they are targeting
- Copy and creative that is resonating
- Visual and copywriting patterns by category
- Recommendations based on observed successful approaches

## Available Output Formats

- Screenshots of ads
- Markdown analysis reports
- CSV spreadsheets
- Presentation decks
- Pattern libraries organized by approach type

## Ethical Guidelines

- Only use for research and inspiration
- Do not directly copy ads or designs
- Insights should inform original creative, not serve as templates for plagiarism
- Respect platform terms of service when accessing ad libraries
