# Tailored Resume Generator

This skill analyzes job postings and customizes resumes to highlight relevant experience, skills, and achievements, maximizing interview chances by optimizing for both human reviewers and ATS systems.

## Primary Use Cases

- Applying for a specific job position
- Customizing your resume for different industries
- Optimizing your resume for ATS systems
- Career transitions
- Creating multiple resume versions

## Key Functionality

1. **Job Analysis**: Analyzes job descriptions to extract requirements and keywords
2. **Priority Identification**: Identifies what the employer values most
3. **Experience Reorganization**: Reorganizes candidate experience to emphasize relevance
4. **ATS Optimization**: Incorporates ATS-friendly language and keywords
5. **Professional Formatting**: Provides formatting with improvement suggestions

## Process Framework

Ten-step methodology:
1. Gather information about the job posting
2. Gather candidate background information
3. Analyze job requirements and extract key criteria
4. Map candidate experience to requirements
5. Prioritize and reorder experience sections
6. Write tailored professional summary
7. Optimize skills section for ATS
8. Quantify achievements with specific metrics
9. Format for readability and ATS compatibility
10. Provide iteration options and suggestions

## Strategic Guidance

- Emphasize truthfulness while presenting qualifications in the best light
- Quantify achievements with specific metrics wherever possible
- Tailor each resume to the specific position rather than using generic templates
- Special considerations for career changers, recent graduates, and technical roles

## Important Note

Review all generated content before submission to ensure accuracy and verify that information appropriately represents your actual qualifications.
